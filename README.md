# TerritorIA · Lo que pasa aquí

Open platform by Equilibria (Peru) where community women leaders use AI to make visible, anonymously and in aggregate, the gender-based violence and sexual and reproductive health needs that are normalized and unreported in their communities. It is not a reporting channel and does not replace existing services (Peru's Línea 100, CEM): it promotes them.

Built on TerritorIA, Equilibria's AI acceleration program for community leaders. Pilot, April 2026: 23 participants from 6 Latin American countries and 20 organizations; 11 certified, each presenting a working AI-built product.

## This demo (synthetic data)
Four accelerators for women leaders in Amazonas, Peru:
1. **Anonymous community diagnostic**: AI suggests non-revictimizing survey questions; responses arrive via WhatsApp, paper or audio; AI transcribes and strikes identifying data before storage; aggregate report names findings per Peruvian Law 30364.
2. **Name it**: local-language radio spots, posters and messages drafted by AI, reviewed by the organization.
3. **Local service route**: district page with existing services (Línea 100, CEM, health post).
4. **Territorial dashboard**: anonymous aggregates by district for health networks; cells with fewer than 5 responses are never published.

AI proposes; leaders and their organizations decide. No individual cases, no identification.

## Run
Static site, no build. Open `index.html` or deploy to any static host (Cloudflare Pages: output directory `/`).

## UNICEF Femtech (Nov 2026)
Proposed for the UNICEF Venture Fund Femtech call. AI outputs in this demo are illustrative examples; the funded work connects generative AI, co-designs the accelerators with women's organizations in Amazonas and validates Awajún translations with native speakers.
